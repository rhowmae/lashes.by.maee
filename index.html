Aftercare

Lash Extension Aftercare
  • Avoid getting lashes wet for the first 24 hours.
  • Clean lashes daily using a lash-safe cleanser.
  • Brush lashes gently with a clean spoolie.
  • Avoid oil-based products around the eyes.
  • Avoid waterproof mascara and curlers.
Retention Tips
  • Wash lashes daily.
  • Brush lashes regularly.
  • Avoid oil-based removers.
  • Schedule fills every 2–3 weeks.
Common Causes of Poor Retention
  • Excessive oils.
  • Touching or rubbing lashes.
  • Using oil-based products.
  • Not cleaning lashes.
